Worn Serpentine Belt

45%

Failing Belt Tensioner

25%

Wheel Bearing Failure

15%

Low Power Steering Fluid

10%

Whining when turning the steering wheel.

Other Causes

5%

Exhaust leaks, loose components, or engine issues.

✅ What You Can Do

  • Check belt condition and tension
  • Top up power steering fluid if low
  • Avoid driving at high speeds if noise worsens
  • Monitor for warning lights

🔧 When to See a Mechanic

  • Grinding that gets louder with speed
  • Loss of power or engine vibration
  • Smoke or overheating alongside the noise
  • Sudden severe knocking
