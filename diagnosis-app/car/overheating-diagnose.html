Possible Causes

Based on your answers

Coolant Leak

45%

Leak in radiator, hose, or water pump.

Thermostat Failure

25%

Stuck closed, preventing coolant flow.

Radiator Fan Issue

15%

Fan not engaging when engine is hot.

Water Pump Failure

10%

Not circulating coolant through the engine.

Other Causes

5%

Low coolant, blocked radiator, or head gasket.

✅ What You Can Do

  • Stop and let the engine cool down
  • Check and top up coolant when cool
  • Check for visible leaks
  • Avoid driving while overheating

🔧 When to See a Mechanic

  • Steam coming from the hood
  • Temperature gauge in the red zone
  • Repeated overheating after refills
  • Low oil or milky oil appearance
