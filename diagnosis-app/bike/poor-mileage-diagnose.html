Possible Causes

Based on your answers

Under-inflated Tyres

40%

Low tyre pressure increases rolling resistance.

Aggressive Riding

25%

Frequent hard acceleration and high RPM.

Clogged Air Filter

15%

Restricting airflow to the engine.

Brake Drag

10%

Brakes not fully releasing, creating resistance.

Other Causes

10%

Worn clutch, dirty fuel system, or chain friction.

✅ What You Can Do

  • Inflate tyres to recommended pressure
  • Clean or replace the air filter
  • Ride smoothly at moderate RPM
  • Lubricate the chain regularly

🔧 When to See a Mechanic

  • Mileage stays low after fixes
  • Brakes or clutch feel faulty
  • Engine sounds rough or misfires
  • Exhaust smoke or fuel smell
