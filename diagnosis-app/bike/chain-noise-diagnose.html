Possible Causes

Dry Chain

45%

Chain Stretch

25%

Chain stretched beyond adjustment limit.

Worn Sprockets

15%

Teeth worn down causing grinding noise.

Rust from Water Exposure

10%

Other Causes

5%

Misalignment, damaged chain link, or debris.

✅ What You Can Do

  • Clean and lubricate the chain
  • Check and adjust chain tension
  • Inspect sprockets for wear
  • Wipe down after rain or washing

🔧 When to See a Mechanic

  • Chain is stretched beyond adjustment
  • Sprocket teeth are severely worn
  • Noise persists after lubrication
  • Chain jumps or slips while riding
