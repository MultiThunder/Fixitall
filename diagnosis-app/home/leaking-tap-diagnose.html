✅ What You Can Do

  • Turn off the water supply first
  • Tighten a loose handle or packing nut
  • Replace a worn washer if dripping
  • Dry the area and check for repeat leaks

🔧 When to Call a Plumber

  • Water pooling under the sink
  • Leak persists after tightening
  • Visible rust or corroded fittings
  • Damp walls or cabinet damage
