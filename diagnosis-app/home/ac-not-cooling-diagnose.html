✅ What You Can Do

  • Clean or replace the air filter
  • Check and reset the thermostat
  • Turn off and defrost a frozen unit
  • Keep vents and condenser clear

🔧 When to Call a Technician

  • No improvement after filter cleaning
  • Suspect a refrigerant leak
  • Water leaking from the indoor unit
  • Unit is old and losing efficiency
