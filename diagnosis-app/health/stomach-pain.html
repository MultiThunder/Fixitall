✅ What You Can Do

  • Oral rehydration solution (ORS)

  • Antacids for burning sensation
  • Small, frequent meals

🚨 Seek Immediate Care If

  • Severe pain in right lower abdomen
  • Vomiting blood or coffee-ground material
  • Black, tarry stools
  • High fever with rigid abdomen
