Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about FixItAll

Is FixItAll free to use?

Yes, FixItAll is completely free. You can use our diagnosis tools for health, automobile, home repairs, and electronics without any charge.

Is the diagnosis accurate?

Our AI-powered system provides probability-based possible causes based on your answers. While we strive for accuracy, the results should be used as a guide – not a definitive diagnosis. Always consult a professional for medical, vehicle, or home repair decisions.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your diagnosis data is processed anonymously and is not linked to your identity. We do not share your personal information with third parties. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

Can I trust the health information?

Our health content is based on verified medical sources and is reviewed by experts. However, it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have a medical emergency, contact a healthcare provider immediately.

How does the diagnosis work?

Simply describe your problem or select a category, then answer a few targeted questions. Our AI analyzes your responses and provides possible causes with probability percentages, along with actionable advice.

Can I suggest a new category or topic?

Absolutely! We're always looking to expand our coverage. Visit our Contact page to send us your suggestions.
